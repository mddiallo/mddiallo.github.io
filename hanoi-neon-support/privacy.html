English

Hanoi Neon works offline. The game does not collect or share personal data and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking or account registration.

Difficulty, language, neon mode and completed-game results (score, disk count, move count and resolution time) are stored only on your device while the app is installed. These records are not uploaded or synced. Uninstalling the app can remove them.

If you contact support, the information you voluntarily include in your email is used to respond to your request.

Contact: diouma.diallo@outlook.com.
